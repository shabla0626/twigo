# Revenue distribution policy: OUTLINE (DRAFT)

> No percentages, figures or promises are set here. The founder must decide the policy
> with legal and tax advice. The public site only states principles.

## Principles (already published on the site)
- Revenue comes only from legitimate customer work.
- Members never pay money to participate.
- Compensation is agreed in writing before work begins.
- Income is not guaranteed.

## Decisions needed
1. **What is distributed:** gross project revenue, revenue after direct costs, or profit?
2. **Order of deductions:** direct project costs, payment fees, taxes, organization
   overhead, reserve.
3. **Organization share:** a fixed or variable share for operations and growth.
4. **Participant shares:** by role, agreed hours, milestones, or a mix. How the share of
   members who leave mid-project is handled.
5. **Idea and research credit:** whether the idea author or the research group receives
   recognition or a share when an idea becomes a paid project.
6. **Timing:** when payments are made (after customer payment clears).
7. **Disputes:** who decides, and how members can appeal.
8. **Transparency:** what members see (see `docs/FINANCIAL-TRANSPARENCY.md`).

Adopt as a versioned document; every project agreement references the version used.

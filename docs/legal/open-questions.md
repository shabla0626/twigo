# Open legal and business questions

These must be answered by the founder with a qualified attorney (and, for tax items, a
CPA). The software keeps the related settings configurable and does not assume answers.

## Before collecting real applicant data publicly
- [ ] **Operator identity** for the privacy notice: legal name, contact address, email.
- [ ] **Privacy notice** updated for member-community data: member profiles, ideas,
      comments, group chat messages, uploaded files, meeting metadata, notifications,
      email delivery logs, session/device records, moderation reports.
- [ ] **Applicable privacy laws** for a U.S. audience (state laws such as CCPA/CPRA if
      thresholds apply; COPPA: the site should state members must be 18+).
- [ ] **Retention periods** per category (configurable at `/admin/settings`); confirm
      defaults or set new ones.
- [ ] **Email provider** data-processing terms.
- [ ] **Age requirement** (recommend 18+; currently not collected or enforced).

## Before any commercial project work
- [ ] **Legal entity** and state of registration (the founder plans to revisit structure
      at about 30 members; **that milestone is not a legal exemption**: if commercial work
      happens earlier, an entity and agreements are needed earlier).
- [ ] **Participant classification**: independent contractors, partners, cooperative
      members or employees. Affects tax forms, minimum wage, liability and insurance.
- [ ] **Project agreement**, confidentiality and IP terms, revenue distribution policy
      (drafts in this folder).
- [ ] **Tax handling**: W-9/1099 for contractors, state requirements, sales tax on services.
- [ ] **Business licenses and insurance** for the activities of approved projects.
- [ ] **Payments**: how customer payments are received and members are paid (the site
      deliberately has no payment, wallet or investment features).

## Community-specific questions
- [ ] Whether research groups could be seen as partnerships or joint ventures, and how
      the rules prevent that (current wording: "voluntary exploration, not a company").
- [ ] Rights to ideas and group research (see confidentiality-and-ip.md).
- [ ] Moderation policy: appeals, data kept from removed content, law-enforcement requests.
- [ ] Rules for financial transparency reports (what can be shared with members).
- [ ] Use of Google Meet: Google's terms apply to members; meetings are not recorded by
      the organization. Decide whether recording is ever allowed (with consent).

# Confidentiality and intellectual-property terms (DRAFT)

> For attorney review. Placeholders in [brackets].

## Confidentiality
- "Confidential information" means non-public information about customers, projects,
  members, finances and unpublished business ideas shared inside a research group or
  project.
- Members use it only for the group or project, and do not disclose it outside without
  written permission. Obligations survive leaving a group or the organization for
  [period].
- Exceptions: information that is public, already known, independently developed, or
  required to be disclosed by law.

## Ideas posted in the community
- Posting an idea in the community shares it with all approved members. [Open question:
  does the author keep any rights to the idea itself? Ideas are generally not protectable;

- Research produced in a group belongs to [the group members jointly / the organization /
  each author] [decision needed]. Recommended: decide before the first group reaches
  "Ready for review".

## Work product from paid projects
- Ownership of deliverables: [assigned to the Organization / to the customer / licensed]
  as stated in each project agreement.
- Members keep their pre-existing knowledge, tools and materials, and grant [a license]
  where they are used in a deliverable.
- No use of an employer's confidential information or work product in any group or
  project.

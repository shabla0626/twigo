# Community rules and code of conduct (DRAFT)

> Source of the published page: `src/app/(site)/community-rules/page.tsx`.
> Keep both in sync. Version: [date of adoption]. Attorney review: [pending].

1. **Purpose.** Members propose, discuss and research ideas for legitimate business
   activities that create real value for paying customers. Ideas, endorsements and
   research groups are voluntary exploration: not companies, jobs, partnerships or
   approved commercial projects.
2. **Paid work.** Paid work starts only after the organization approves a project and
   everyone involved signs a written agreement with clear scope and compensation terms.
3. **Money rules.** No one may request membership fees, joining fees, deposits, training
   payments, capital contributions or investments; no recruitment-based earnings; no
   requests for bank details, Social Security numbers, ID documents or passwords.
4. **Respect and honesty.** No harassment or discrimination. No exaggerated income or
   profit claims.
5. **Confidentiality and IP.** No confidential information from employers or clients.
   Private group content stays in the group. Project work-product ownership follows the
   project agreement.
6. **Privacy.** Share only what you are comfortable sharing. Never share another
   member's contact details or messages. Do not record meetings without consent.
7. **Lawful activity only.** No deceptive, gambling, unlicensed financial or other
   regulated activity without the required licenses.
8. **Moderation.** Reports go to administrators. They may remove content and suspend
   groups or accounts; significant actions are audited. Administrators do not browse
   private chats; they see a message only when a member reports it, and see a group's
   research notes when the group asks for a proposal review.
